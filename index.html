Hello, I'm Joanna

Think of me as a good friend who happens to plan extraordinary trips.

I built Joanna Elizabeth Travel for the way I love to travel: slowly, intentionally, and with all the small details considered long before you ever pick up a suitcase. Every itinerary I design is built around you — your pace, your people, your way of seeing the world.

I'm easy to reach, easy to talk to, and genuinely invested in your trip. When you work with me, you get a real person on the other end of every email — one who has either been where you're going or knows exactly who to call.

Based in Boston, I'm a lifelong competitive equestrian, a mom of three boys, an enthusiastic lover of food and wine, and a sucker for a great beach. When I'm not planning your trip, I'm probably plotting my own.
