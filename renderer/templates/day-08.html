A Slow Day in Oia

Your last full day · spent exactly the way it should be
  1. Morning
    OIA
    An open Sunday at Andronis Arcadia
    No scheduled activities. Breakfast on the suite terrace, a long swim, time at the Evexia Spa — the day is yours to do nothing in particular.
  2. Midday
    OIA
    Lunch on your own
    Plenty of options walking distance from the property — Lotza for the caldera view, Sunset Taverna at Ammoudi Bay if you'd like the seafood-and-stairs ritual. Concierge can book on the spot.
  3. Sunset
    OIA
    Sunset stroll through Oia
    The famous sunset stroll. Walk the caldera edge from the Castro out toward the windmill — get there 60 minutes before sunset to claim a viewing spot, or watch from your suite terrace if the village feels too busy.
  4. Evening
    OIA
    Last dinner of the trip
    Dinner is open. Beefbar Santorini is on-property; for one last cliff-edge meal, Lauda or Ambrosia in Oia are easy walks. Concierge will lock anything in.
