Le Sirenuse

A Sersale family villa overlooking the Tyrrhenian since 1951.

Set into the cliffs of Positano, Le Sirenuse has remained quietly the finest address on the coast for three generations. Sixty-three rooms and suites are spread across the original villa's ochre façade, each dressed in hand-painted Vietri tiles and Sersale family antiques.

The Champagne & Oyster bar opens to the sea at sunset; La Sponda — the only Michelin-starred restaurant in Positano — is set with a thousand candles each evening. Mornings begin with espresso on the terrace and the church bell of Santa Maria below.
