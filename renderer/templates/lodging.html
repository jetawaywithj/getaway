Le Sirenuse

A Sersale family villa overlooking the Tyrrhenian since 1951.
Sixty-three rooms set into the cliffs of Positano, each dressed in hand-painted Vietri tiles. The Champagne & Oyster bar opens to the sea at sunset; La Sponda — the only Michelin-starred restaurant in town — is set with a thousand candles each evening.
