Welcome to Greece!

Joanna, this is going to be such a beautiful week.

Athens, Mykonos, Paros, Santorini — four very different islands strung together over seven days, with the kind of pace that lets you actually breathe between them. A whitewashed start in Plaka, two nights tucked into a private villa above Psarou, an organic farm lunch on Paros, and the caldera of Oia as a finale.

La Divina is your home in Athens — a 12-suite neoclassical hideaway dedicated to Maria Callas, with views straight to the Acropolis. From there, a quick flight to Mykonos and your private villa. Ferries handle the island hops to Paros and Santorini, with private vans waiting at every port and airport, every single day.

Lunches and dinners are pre-arranged at the spots worth pre-arranging — the Cook & Feast chef night at the Mykonos villa, the farm tasting on Paros, the wine tour through Santorini's volcanic vineyards, the farewell at Pacman as the sun sets over the caldera. The mornings are mostly yours to slow down with.

The rhythm is intentional: two nights in the islands you'll want to linger in, one night where one is enough. Transfers are confirmed door-to-door, ferries are booked, and every property is expecting you.

If anything shifts on the ground — a ferry running late, a restaurant closing early, a beach club you'd rather skip — your local concierge and I are both a text away. The whole point is that you don't have to think about logistics.

Have the most wonderful trip.
